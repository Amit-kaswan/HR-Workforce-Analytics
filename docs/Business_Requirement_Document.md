# 1. Business Background

The Human Resources (HR) department is responsible for managing employee information and supporting workforce planning. As organizations grow, manually analyzing employee data becomes time-consuming and makes it difficult to identify important workforce trends.

An interactive analytics dashboard can help HR monitor key workforce metrics and make faster, data-driven decisions.

---

# 2. Business Problem

The HR department does not have a centralized dashboard to monitor important workforce metrics such as employee count, attrition, salary distribution, department performance, gender diversity, and workforce distribution across different locations.

As a result, HR managers spend more time preparing reports and have limited visibility into workforce trends.

---

# 3. Business Objectives

The dashboard should help HR teams to:

- Monitor workforce size.
- Track employee attrition.
- Analyze salary distribution.
- Monitor employee demographics.
- Compare departments.
- Support workforce planning.
- Make data-driven decisions.

---

# 4. Key Business Questions

The dashboard should answer the following questions:

- How many employees are currently working?
- How many employees have left the company?
- What is the overall attrition rate?
- Which department has the highest attrition?
- What is the average salary?
- What is the average employee experience?
- How are employees distributed by gender?
- How are employees distributed across different cities?

---

# 5. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Employees
- Employees Left
- Attrition Rate
- Average Salary
- Average Experience
- Average Age

---

# 6. Dashboard Requirements

The dashboard should include:

- KPI Cards
- Department-wise Employee Distribution
- Department-wise Attrition Analysis
- Gender Distribution
- Salary Analysis
- City-wise Employee Distribution
- Interactive Filters (Slicers)

---

# 7. Expected Business Outcome

The dashboard should enable HR managers to:

- Monitor workforce health.
- Identify departments with high attrition.
- Improve workforce planning.
- Support employee retention initiatives.
- Make evidence-based decisions.

---

# 8. Assumptions & Limitations

The current dataset helps identify workforce trends and highlights areas that require attention. However, additional information such as employee feedback, exit interview responses, overtime, workload, promotion history, and expected salary versus actual salary would be required to determine the root causes of attrition.
