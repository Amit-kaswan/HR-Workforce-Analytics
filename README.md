# 📊 HR Workforce Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive HR Workforce Analytics Dashboard built using **Excel, SQL, and Power BI**. It analyzes employee data to uncover workforce trends, employee attrition, salary distribution, workforce demographics, and city-wise employee distribution.

The objective of this project is to demonstrate how data analytics can help HR teams make informed decisions related to hiring, employee retention, workforce planning, and organizational performance.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

HR teams need quick access to workforce insights to make informed decisions. Without a centralized dashboard, analyzing employee attrition, salary distribution, workforce demographics, and employee distribution across multiple cities can be time-consuming and inefficient.

This dashboard addresses these challenges by providing interactive visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs) that support strategic HR decision-making.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators

- Total Employees
- Employees Left
- Overall Attrition Rate
- Average Salary
- Average Experience
- Average Age

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Workforce is well distributed across multiple cities.
- Gender distribution is balanced, indicating healthy workforce diversity.
- Most employees fall within the 5L–10L salary band.
- Finance department has the highest attrition rate, while HR has the lowest.
- Workforce distribution supports efficient workforce planning.

---

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

- Investigate the root causes of attrition in the Finance department.
- Continue the current hiring strategy to maintain balanced workforce distribution.
- Benchmark salary structures regularly against industry standards.
- Monitor employee retention and workforce diversity using HR KPIs.
- Review workforce trends periodically to support business planning.
